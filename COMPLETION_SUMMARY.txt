💡 PRO TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📸 REGISTRATION TIPS
   √ Use good lighting
   √ Capture varied angles
   √ Different expressions help
   √ 15-20 images is ideal
   √ Move face around during capture

✅ ATTENDANCE MARKING TIPS
   √ Keep camera at eye level
   √ Good lighting is important
   √ Position face in the circle
   √ Remain still while capturing
   √ Similar lighting to registration

🔧 TROUBLESHOOTING TIPS
   √ Clear browser cache if styles don't load
   √ Check F12 console for errors
   √ Verify both servers are running
   √ Check camera permissions
   √ Restart servers if stuck
